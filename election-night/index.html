Interpretation · not a projection

Connecting to live counts. Democrats' first priority is flipping enough Republican-held House seats to take the gavel; the Senate demands a harder, multi-state path.

House: Control unlocks oversight, hearings, subpoenas, budget leverage, and blocking new legislation. Not automatic repeal of existing executive actions.
Senate: Control would bring committee leadership and confirmation leverage. The filibuster still constrains much ordinary legislation.
Counting: First returns are not representative samples. Always examine vote-counting geography and ballot type before reading a swing.

How this works, and what it does not claim

Federal vote counts: official state election-office results redistributed by Open America (22-state coverage) and, when accessible, The People's Tally (independent publicly available JSON from state feeds). Expected vote percentages are not identical to percent of precincts or counties reporting. We do not substitute one for the other.

Desk calls: The People's Tally's separately published result snapshots, only when they identify a decision desk and a winner. No candidate is treated as a called winner merely for leading. For all races, AP elections coverage and the race-call board are direct cross-checks. Official certification comes later from states.

Washington Supreme Court contests and initiatives: link to the Secretary of State. Because a verified browser-readable structured feed for those contests was not available, you can enter vote totals manually using Edit. Such entries are visibly labeled manual and stored only in this browser. They are never called automatically.

Updates stop while this tab is closed; this is a browser dashboard, not a notification service. Cross-origin restrictions or provider outages can prevent live loading: the app will show that rather than manufacturing results. Data checked timestamp is the browser-request time; the count timestamp is separately identified when supplied by a source.
